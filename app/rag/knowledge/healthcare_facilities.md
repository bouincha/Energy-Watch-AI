# Energy in Healthcare Facilities

## Constraints
Hospitals and clinics operate 24/7 and have strict requirements for air changes,
temperature and humidity in operating rooms and intensive care. Patient safety always
takes priority over energy savings, so changes to HVAC in clinical areas require approval
from the facility manager.

## Opportunities
Set back HVAC in administrative and outpatient areas at night, optimize domestic hot water
temperature while respecting Legionella prevention (keep storage above 60 °C), switch
medical imaging equipment to standby mode when idle, and maintain chillers to avoid faults.
