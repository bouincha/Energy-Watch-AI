# Lighting Efficiency

## LED retrofit
Replacing fluorescent tubes or metal-halide high-bay lamps with LED saves 40 to 70 % of
lighting energy with a typical payback of 1 to 3 years.

## Controls
Occupancy sensors in meeting rooms, toilets and storage areas save 20 to 40 %. Daylight
harvesting near windows and skylights saves another 15 to 30 %.

## Lighting left on outside working hours
Lighting consumption during weekends or nights in an office building indicates missing or
overridden time schedules, a failed lighting controller, or cleaning crews switching
everything on. Corrective action: program a time schedule in the lighting controller,
add a "sweep-off" command every evening at 20:00 and on weekends, and keep only safety
and emergency lighting active.

## Measurement
Track lighting power density (W/m²); efficient offices are below 6-8 W/m².
